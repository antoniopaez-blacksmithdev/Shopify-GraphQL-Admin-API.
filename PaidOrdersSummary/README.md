# PaidOrdersSummary

## Español 🇪🇸

Revisar rápidamente los pedidos pagados ayuda a detectar ventas recientes, comprobar importes y enlazar cada compra con su cliente. Con **PaidOrdersSummary** puedes consultar ese resumen aplicando la sintaxis de búsqueda de pedidos de Shopify.

- **Connection: QueryRoot** — punto de entrada de la consulta GraphQL.
- **Object: Order** — representa una compra realizada en la tienda.
- **Field: displayFinancialStatus** — estado financiero presentado al comerciante.
- **Object: MoneyBag** — contiene importes en moneda de la tienda y de presentación.
- **Field: customer** — cliente asociado al pedido, si existe.
- **Scopes:** `read_orders`, `read_marketplace_orders`, `read_quick_sale`, `read_customers`.
- **URL del objeto elegido:** [Order](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/2026-04/objects/Order)

## English 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁥󠁮󠁧󠁿

Quickly reviewing paid orders helps identify recent sales, verify amounts, and link each purchase to its customer. With **PaidOrdersSummary**, you can request that summary while applying Shopify order search syntax.

The randomly selected element is **Order**, with a specific focus on the financial summary:

- **Connection: QueryRoot** — the entry point for the GraphQL query.
- **Object: Order** — represents a purchase made in the shop.
- **Field: displayFinancialStatus** — the financial status shown to the merchant.
- **Object: MoneyBag** — contains amounts in shop and presentment currencies.
- **Field: customer** — the customer associated with the order, when present.
- **Scopes:** `read_orders`, `read_marketplace_orders`, `read_quick_sale`, `read_customers`.
- **URL of the selected object:** [Order](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/2026-04/objects/Order)
